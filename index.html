Ask a question

Does Metanor store patient records?

No. Bookings and patient records live on the licensed booking platform your clinic chooses, in the Kingdom where required under the PDPL. We build the website, connect the platform and train your staff.

What does it cost?

The website is free. Everything else we go through with you, based on what your clinic needs.

Which booking platform will we use?

One your clinic chooses, with our help. In Saudi Arabia there are licensed platforms such as Watily and Clinicy. A WhatsApp number can only connect to one platform, so in a live setup the WhatsApp chat usually comes from the booking platform.

Does the WhatsApp chat give medical advice?

No. It can suggest a department and say when to get urgent help. Medical questions go to your staff.

Are the demos linked to real patients?

No. Both demos run on made-up data and connect to no clinic, WhatsApp account or patient record. The WhatsApp demo shows the patient experience.
